## 4. Industry trends and forecasts

### 4.1 Market Size

| Forecasting Agency | 2025 | 2030 |
|---------|--------|--------|
| McKinsey (Global) | US$128 billion | US$156 billion |
| Guosheng Securities (China) | 167.5 billion yuan | 375.9 billion yuan |
| Among them: smart cockpit domain control | 34.9 billion yuan | 40.4 billion yuan |
| Among them: autonomous driving domain control | 87.1 billion yuan | 160.3 billion yuan |

### 4.2 Key Trends

1. **Self-research and outsourcing coexist**: Horizon founder Yu Kai judged that **20% of car companies choose self-research, and 80% still choose third-party cooperation**
2. **Accelerating domestic substitution**: Among the top ten suppliers of cockpit domain control, seven are domestically produced (52.7%); the top 3 smart driving domain control suppliers are all local companies (66.2%)
3. **Cabin-driving integration**: Single domain control → The trend of central computing platform is clear, Desay SV IPU14 (Thor) takes the lead in mass production
4. **Smart driving sinks**: In January 2026, the smart driving domain control assembly rate has reached 28.7%, the monthly penetration rate has exceeded 30% for the first time, and the NOA function has dropped from 200,000+ to 100,000 levels.
5. **Chip localization**: Qualcomm’s cockpit share dropped from 75.9% to 72.1%, and the total of Xinqing/Huawei/Xinchi increased to ~15%
